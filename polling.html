01The loop

One loop, run for the life of the flight.

Most polling is a report that arrives after the argument. This one stays in the field.

  1. Field the question

    Pick it from the library, or write it for your race.

  2. Answers stream back

    Over 1 million a day; the read builds in days, not field weeks.

  3. Build the audience

    From what people actually said, every question named.

  4. Activate

    A deal ID in the DSP seat you already buy through.

  5. Read delivery

    Your delivery beside their spend, labeled, never blurred.

  6. Re-field and optimize

    Ask again, read what moved, shift the buy.

Included with a media investment, your DSP seat or ours

Declared first. Modeled only if you ask.

The seed stays declared, the expansion is modeled, and we say which is which. A modeled lookalike extension is optional and always labeled modeled, and the declared core does not decay the way a modeled lookalike does: by some industry estimates, a lookalike model sheds 30 to 60 percent of its lift within 90 days of being built.

CivicScience's case studies show declared audiences outperforming third-party controls: 36 to 80 percent higher click-through and 45 to 62 percent higher conversion in matched commercial tests. Those are the partner's case studies, not a promise about any single flight.

Run the media on a KPI plan, your seat or ours, and both come with it. Standalone research is by rate card, on request.
