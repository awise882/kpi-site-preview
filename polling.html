How the audience holds up

The declared seed

Every audience starts from what people actually answered — never inferred interest, never the same rented voter file every side is buying. Different inputs find different people, so the reach is net-new — and the questions that built it are named, auditable line by line.

The licensed spine

Experian-class consumer attributes carry every profile to census benchmarks — used only for the weighting and the identifier match.

The freshness window

Answers age out on a stated window and the audience rebuilds from what people are saying now — instead of decaying like a modeled lookalike. The through-date prints on every read, and it ships as a deal ID with no onboarding hop to bleed it out.

The check before the dollar

Every audience ships with a composition read and its effective n before the IO — you see what you are buying while it is still refusable. Anything modeled sits apart, labeled.

The full weighting spec — benchmarks, windows, and the QA gates — opens in the walkthrough: enough to audit us, not enough to copy us.
