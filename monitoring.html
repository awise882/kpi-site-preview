Stop 03 · as stations post · FCC rates

What a spot costs, from the public file.

Station-level political rates, read off public FCC filings — what stations charged, by daypart and week. Both sides of the rate conversation arrive holding the same file.

What subscribers pull

  • Rate history — by station, daypart and week; FCC-verified where filed
  • Flight over flight — the same daypart, week by week, dated
  • Last cycle against this one — same station, same daypart, same week of the calendar
  • Cleared vs quoted — the competitive buy’s actual clearances against your quote
  • Class vs market — candidates and outside groups read separately, LUR-protected
  • Sellers pull it too — to defend the rate

The rate book runs all 210 markets, flight over flight — the $12K clawback it prices is worked through in the buyer’s week; the seller’s Friday holds $17K with the same file.
