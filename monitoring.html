Streaming & Digital

The money with no paper trail, tracked anyway.

The streaming buy never touches a public file, so we capture it on the screen itself — and read it service by service, priced as a dated, estimated minimum.

A third of measured political TV ad impressions are streaming — beyond the reach of transparency reports and public ad libraries, measured here on the glass. 220M+ streaming ad impressions measured in panel households in the peak summer week (week of August 3, 2026) — a hard floor, observed on real screens in real homes · 1.06B measured across the six summer weeks (July 15 – August 29, 2026).
  • ~940M implied national streaming ad impressions in that same peak week — ~4.5B across the six weeks · implied via our ratified capture calibration, always labeled
  • ~10M individual ad plays observed per week, app by app: YouTube and YouTube TV, Tubi, Peacock, Pluto, the vMVPDs
  • 1,500+ active political advertisers measured in a single week · YouTube and YouTube TV carry nearly half of measured streaming
  • Set before the money arrived: these weeks ran while less than 10% of projected cycle dollars had been spent. Fall projects to 3–4B+ implied national streaming impressions per week at peak, of which about a quarter is directly observed at household grain (projection; assumes streaming volume scales with spend)

Measured and implied never blend: measured is the floor, panel-observed on our smart-TV measurement panel; implied is the national read, labeled · settled through August 29, 2026 — trailing weeks publish only once they settle.

Every ad is caught on the glass — 100% of the creatives that run there, each with tape and transcript — and priced as a dated, estimated minimum, service by service: YouTube and YouTube TV, Tubi, Peacock, Pluto, the vMVPDs.

The measured set, in plain terms: whatever renders on the TV glass in the 40 million measured homes — YouTube and YouTube TV, the premium ad-supported apps, free ad-supported TV, the vMVPDs — read service by service, rolled up by platform class. Why the estimates hold

955M+ political streaming impressions, log-verified — logged and reconciled since January 2025. Straight from the server log.
  • 3.5M a day in August 2026 — 24M+ a week, up from 2.1M a day across 2026
  • 98.0% completion — 918.9M spots watched to the last frame, 7.5 million hours of finished viewing
  • 608 of 608 days with log-verified streaming — every single day since January 2025
  • 288 distinct apps in a single month — and that count is a floor, not the total

The estimates above are calibrated against nearly a billion log-verified streaming impressions — the market’s streaming is detected on the glass; the calibration set lives on the server log.
