Streaming & Digital

The money with no paper trail, tracked anyway.

The streaming buy never touches a public file, so we capture it on the screen itself — and read it service by service, priced as a dated, estimated minimum.

A third of measured political TV ad impressions are streaming — beyond the reach of transparency reports and public ad libraries, measured here on the glass. 220M+ streaming ad impressions measured in the peak summer week (week of August 3, 2026) · 1.06B measured across the six summer weeks.
  • ~940M implied national streaming ad impressions in that same peak week — ~4.5B across the six weeks · implied by weighting what the panel measured to the national household universe — always labeled
  • 1,500+ active political advertisers measured in a single week · YouTube and YouTube TV carry nearly half of measured streaming

Measured and implied never blend: measured is the floor, panel-observed on our smart-TV measurement panel; implied is the national read, labeled · settled through August 29, 2026 — trailing weeks publish only once they settle.

The number on this board

Every ad is caught on the glass — 100% of the creatives that run there, each with tape and transcript — and priced as a dated, estimated minimum, service by service: YouTube and YouTube TV, Tubi, Peacock, Pluto, the vMVPDs.

The measured set, in plain terms

The measured set: whatever renders on the TV glass in the 40 million measured homes, read service by service and rolled up by platform class. A play is one captured airing on one measured screen; impressions weight plays to the household universe. Don’t see your platform named? The class roster opens on the walkthrough.
