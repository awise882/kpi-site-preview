The call list · the win-back call

Every call comes with its reason

The forward book ranks the list: money committed to the market that has not reached your books is the first conversation, and one order recaptured from it is the business case for the year. The went-dark row is the other call: who stopped, when, and what was still booked when they did. A conversation with a reason, never a cold call.
