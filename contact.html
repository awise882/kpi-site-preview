What happens next

Most of the work happens before the call.

A product tour can be run on anything. A walkthrough of your race cannot, which is why the pull comes first and the screen share comes second. Timing depends on the week, and in the loud stretch of a cycle it takes longer. We will tell you where it sits rather than book a slot we cannot prepare for.

  1. We reply with a question, not a calendar

    We confirm the race or state, the office, and who should be on the call. If the contest is quiet enough that the screen would be thin, we say so before you spend the time.

  2. Your race gets built in advance

    Spend by lane and market, the creative with transcripts, and the booking horizon, each carrying its own through-date. Where coverage is thin, the screen says insufficient signal.

  3. You judge it against what you already know

    You have watched this contest for months. Check the numbers against that. If it confirms what you had and adds nothing, that is a real answer and worth knowing early.
