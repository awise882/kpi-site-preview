Privacy

What we collect, and what we do with it.

This site asks for information in one place: the demo request form and the email addresses beside it. Here is the whole story, stated the same way we state a spend figure.

What we collect

What you type into the demo request form or send us by email: your name, work email, organization, role, the race or state you want pulled, and what you are trying to solve. Nothing is collected silently; this site sets no advertising trackers.

What we use it for

To prepare and run your walkthrough and to reply to you. The recipient is Kinetiq Political Insights. It goes to the analysts who run the walkthroughs, not into a marketing list.

What we never do with it

We do not sell it. We do not share it with a campaign or a committee. We work both sides of the aisle, and what one prospect tells us is not available to another.

The subscriber firewall

What subscribers do inside the product stays inside their seat. Subscriber queries, saved races, saved audiences, delivery reports and exports are never visible to or used by any media-buying engagement other than your own, and no buyer's media plan feeds the competitive surfaces. The one exception is the rate calibration, taken with written consent: invoice rates from media we placed ourselves calibrate the market rate ranges, rates only and in aggregate, never plans, placements or delivery.

How long we keep it

While we are in contact with you about a walkthrough, and no longer than 24 months from your last message to us.

Your choices

Write to info@kpipolitical.com to see what we hold, correct it, or have it deleted. Deletion on request, no questions asked.

As of August 2026. If this notice changes, the change is dated here, the same way a restatement is.
