Privacy

What we collect, and what we do with it.

This site collects two things: what you send us through the walkthrough request form, and basic usage data. The whole story, stated the same way we state a spend figure.

What we collect

What you type into the walkthrough request form or send us by email — name, work email, organization, role, the race or market you want pulled — and basic usage data: pages viewed, device and browser type.

What we use it for

To run your walkthrough and reply to you, and to operate and improve the site. We and our service providers may use cookies and similar technologies to measure how the site is used; as those tools change, this notice is updated here.

What we never do with it

We do not sell it, and we do not share it with a campaign or a committee. We work both sides of the aisle.

Your choices

Write to info@kpipolitical.com to see what we hold, correct it, or have it deleted — kept no longer than 24 months either way.

As of September 2026. If this notice changes, the change is dated here, the same way a restatement is.
