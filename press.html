August 30, 2026 · Forecast update

KPI forecasts $10.4 billion in 2026 political ad spending.

Kinetiq Political Insights projects $10.4 billion in 2026 political advertising across broadcast, cable, radio, and streaming & digital — an update to the April 2026 forecast of $10.0 billion, revised upward on posted orders and forward reservations through August 30, 2026.

$1.2 billion in forward reservations is already on file across the 209 federal and state races with reservations posted, with 303 races carrying posted or detected money today. The forecast rides the same dataset as the platform: 1.5 million ad occurrences monitored per day at real costs — posted orders and invoices, not rate-card math. How streaming is measured is the partnership announcement below.

"The number moves as the orders post — that is the point. Anyone quoting a projection should be able to watch it update."

Adam Wise, Kinetiq Political Insights

August 2026 · Partnership

KPI announces streaming measurement across 40 million households, in partnership with VideoAmp.

Kinetiq Political Insights now measures political streaming advertising where it runs — on the TV screen — through automatic content recognition across 40 million U.S. households and 65 million devices of smart-TV viewing data, in partnership with VideoAmp.

Streaming spots are captured with tape, platform, and transcript, including buys that never touch broadcast. Impression measurement covers the top 58 markets by households; monitoring runs in all 210. Modeled shares print as dated estimates.

"Streaming was the hole in every political buy sheet. Measuring it on the glass, in the home, closes it — without ever touching a platform's logs."

Adam Wise, Kinetiq Political Insights
